TV Probe (for testers)

Checks what your LG TV offers to apps and writes a report, so support for older webOS versions (such as webOS 4) can be planned from facts instead of test builds. It changes nothing on the TV.

  • webOS, glibc and kernel versions, and the app sandbox
  • Every library and symbol the Chromium Beta browser needs, and which ones this TV lacks
  • Wayland and the TV's webOS protocol tables, the GPU (with a test draw), LG's hardware video libraries (NDL, Starfish/ACB, LGNC), memory
  • Optional: a 12 s hardware video test on webOS 4 (bin/run ndltest as root)

How to use

Open the app once and wait for the message. The report is in /tmp/org.nativechromium.tvprobe/report.txt. Attach it to an issue on github.com/sarahahzub/WebOS-Chromium with your TV model.
