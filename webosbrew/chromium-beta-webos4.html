Chromium Beta WebOS 4 (for webOS 4 testers)

The first test build of WebOS Chromium for webOS 4.4 TVs. It's a separate app with its own icon, settings and bookmarks. It doesn't change Chromium or Chromium Beta, and you can remove it any time. On webOS 5 and newer, use Chromium Beta instead.

What's being tested in 0.0.54

  • Built for glibc 2.24 (webOS 4.4), with its own NSS and NSPR libraries, which webOS 4 doesn't have.
  • Works without the Wayland interfaces webOS 4 lacks.
  • Hardware video through LG's older NDL decoder: sites are offered only H.264 (up to 1080p30). If that fails, it switches to software decoding (720p).

Please report

Your TV model, webOS version, and whether it starts, opens websites, plays YouTube and works with the remote. With root: media.log, browser.log and start-check.log from /tmp/org.nativechromium.browser.webos4/. Report on github.com/sarahahzub/WebOS-Chromium (mention "Beta WebOS 4 0.0.54").
