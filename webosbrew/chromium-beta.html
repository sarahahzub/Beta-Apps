Chromium Beta (for testers)

A test build of WebOS Chromium. It installs next to the normal Chromium app with its own icon, settings and bookmarks, so it can't break your normal install. Remove it any time.

What's being tested in 0.0.47

  • Older TVs: this build is meant to run on webOS 5 (2020) and newer; the normal app needs webOS 9.
  • Fixed: the app closing right after the splash screen (e.g. LG C1, webOS 6).
  • Hardware video, GPU drawing and the remote on those older models.

Please report

Your TV model, webOS version, and whether it starts, plays YouTube smoothly and works with the remote: github.com/sarahahzub/WebOS-Chromium (TV compatibility report, mention "Beta 0.0.47").
